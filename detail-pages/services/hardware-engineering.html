Overview

From breadboard to a board that ships.

We design the electronics behind the product — schematics, PCB layout and the firmware that brings the board to life, tested on real hardware rather than only in simulation.

You get working prototypes you can hold, documented designs your manufacturer can build from, and firmware that is ready for the field.

What's included

Everything the engagement covers

PCB Design

Schematics and multi-layer board layout, ready for the fab house.

Firmware

Embedded C and Rust on bare metal or RTOS, written to be debuggable.

Prototyping

Fast iterations you can hold — from breadboard to assembled sample.

Sensor Integration

Reading the physical world reliably, and getting the data off-board.

Power & Signal

Power budgets, regulation and signal integrity that survive the real world.

Testing & Validation

Bring-up, bench measurement and stress testing before it goes to production.

Process

How we work

01 Requirements We pin down what the board must do, its power budget and its constraints.
02 Schematic Part selection and circuit design, reviewed before anything is fabricated.
03 Layout PCB routing with power and signal integrity designed in from the start.
04 Bring-up We assemble the first boards, power them up and write the firmware.
05 Validate Bench testing and revisions until the design is ready to manufacture.
