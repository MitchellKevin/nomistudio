Overview

Find the weaknesses before someone else does.

We stress-test your stack the way a real attacker would — probing your applications, infrastructure and code for weaknesses before they can ever be exploited.

You get a clear, prioritised report with practical fixes, plus hands-on help hardening your systems and staying compliant.

Process

How we work

01 Scope We agree on targets, rules of engagement and clear objectives.
02 Assess We map your attack surface and probe for vulnerabilities.
03 Exploit We safely validate findings to confirm real, exploitable risk.
04 Report We deliver a prioritised report with clear, actionable fixes.
05 Remediate We support your team through the fixes and re-test to confirm.
